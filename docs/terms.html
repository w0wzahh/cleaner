What the Software does #

Cleaner is a utility that identifies and, at your explicit direction, permanently deletes files and folders from your computer. That is its purpose. Depending on your settings it may:

  • Move files to the Windows recycle bin (default behavior),
  • Permanently delete files, or
  • Securely overwrite files (3-pass erase), which is irreversible.

You are responsible for reviewing every scan preview before confirming a clean, choosing appropriate filters and directories, configuring the protected-paths list for anything you can't afford to lose, and maintaining your own backups.
